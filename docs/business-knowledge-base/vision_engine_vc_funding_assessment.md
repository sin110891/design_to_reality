## What the evaluation proves—and does not prove

It proves:

- your curated design knowledge improves model output
- Lloyd’s judgement is being converted into reusable system intelligence
- the dataset may create a genuine quality advantage
- the feedback loop could compound over time

It does **not** yet prove:

- customers will pay
- the product saves enough time or money
- outputs are consistently usable in commercial projects
- the advantage survives across thousands of rooms and styles
- competitors cannot reproduce it

That gap is what determines funding.

## What the system is actually worth

The current system is not principally valuable because it generates attractive images.

Its deeper value is:

> **It converts taste, design reasoning and expert correction into a repeatable decision system.**

That can evolve from:

1. inspiration generation  
2. concept approval  
3. material selection  
4. scope creation  
5. quote preparation  
6. supplier and trade routing  
7. eventual project outcome learning  

The image is the entry point. The valuable business is the workflow and data underneath it.

## How to create huge customer value

The strongest product promise is not:

> “We make better AI interiors.”

It is:

> “We help design and renovation firms reach an approved, commercially usable concept faster, with fewer revisions and a clearer route to quotation.”

Target measurable pain:

- reduce concept development from days to hours
- reduce client revision cycles
- increase design proposal conversion
- reduce pre-sales labour
- move from concept to material scope faster
- help non-design staff create architect-controlled proposals

Vertical AI adoption has been strongest where it replaces expensive, manual, unstructured professional work and demonstrates clear ROI. ([menlovc.com](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/))

## What maximises funding probability

### 1. Add commercial proof

Run paid pilots with:

- interior-design studios
- kitchen and bathroom firms
- design-and-build companies
- small architecture practices
- premium renovation businesses

Target before fundraising:

- **5–10 paying pilots**
- **£3k–£10k MRR**
- 60%+ weekly active pilot usage
- 30–50% reduction in concept turnaround time
- measurable reduction in revisions
- at least 2 customers willing to provide references

With this, I would estimate pre-seed VC probability rises toward **50–70%**, assuming the product and team are credible.

### 2. Narrow the wedge

Do not initially claim all rooms, styles and users.

Start with something like:

> Contemporary residential living rooms and kitchens for small UK design-and-build firms.

Narrow scope improves quality, evaluation and sales clarity.

### 3. Demonstrate the data flywheel

Show investors:

```text id="m069df"
customer project
→ generated alternatives
→ architect/client selection
→ corrections and rejection reasons
→ improved retrieval and ranking
→ higher acceptance and fewer retries
```

Prove that performance improves as reviewed data grows.

### 4. Connect concepts to revenue

Investors will value the company much more once the Vision Engine leads into:

- materials
- work packages
- quote-ready scopes
- supplier options
- contractor bids

That moves you from an image tool into infrastructure for renovation commerce.

PropTech capital recovered in 2025, but investors concentrated on durable, capital-efficient businesses tied to real-world economics. ([creti.org](https://creti.org/insights/proptech-venture-capital-in-2025-end-of-year-report))

## Recommended funding strategy

Raise an initial **£500k–£900k pre-seed** for roughly 12–18 months.

Use it to achieve:

- 20–30 paying design businesses
- £15k–£30k MRR
- validated concept-to-quote workflow
- 5,000–15,000 reviewed design decisions
- evidence that customer feedback improves acceptance rates
- one narrow category with clear repeat usage

At that stage, a **£2m–£4m seed round** becomes materially more credible.

## Bottom line

The blinded evaluation could make the company **fundable at angel or specialist pre-seed level**.

To become strongly VC-fundable, you must prove three things together:

1. **Better design outputs**
2. **Meaningful business ROI**
3. **A compounding proprietary data loop**

The biggest opportunity is not becoming the best AI rendering tool. It is becoming the **design-intelligence and commercial decision layer between inspiration and completed renovation**.
