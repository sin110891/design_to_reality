# Commissions

### How do commissions work?

Channel3 has partnered with thousands of brands who pay commission on AI-driven sales.

After your agent recommends a product, we track if the end-user buys it (within a retailer-set timeframe). We pay you for purchases.

You can see estimated commission rates on the Channel3 dashboard and in the product search response.

### Why do rates show "maximum"?

Commissions can be adjusted or canceled by retailers for reasons such as returns, order cancellations, suspected fraud, attribution to another channel, or network quality concerns.

### When and how are commissions paid out?

Earnings appear on the [Channel3 dashboard](https://trychannel3.com/dashboard) once attributed sales clear the retailer's return window. Earnings are paid out securely via Stripe.
